Do / Don't

Do
  • Use chips for quick, contextual AI prompts
  • Use cards when the recommendation needs rationale
  • Limit chips to 4 max per context
  • Show "Applied" confirmation with green tint
  • Keep dismissed cards visible but faded
Don't
  • Use cards for simple navigation tasks (use chips)
  • Show more than 4 chips — causes decision paralysis
  • Use chips without the Sparkle prefix
  • Auto-dismiss cards without user action
  • Mix chip and card styles in the same row

Guidelines & Animation

AI Color & Spec Guidelines

The Ruby Pivot: A Study in Contrast

Ruby Berry was chosen to ensure Glow remains instantly recognizable within the existing Rosewood interface.

High-Visibility Anchoring
Ruby creates a natural visual tension against our primary blues, making AI entry points discoverable without adding UI clutter. The contrast ratio between Ruby Berry (#C0429F) and our dashboard blue ensures WCAG AA compliance while maintaining brand distinction.
Warmth & Human-Centricity
Ruby Berry aligns with the luxury and wellness industries we serve, lending the AI a more sophisticated, approachable persona. This warmth signals that Glow is a partner, not a calculator.
Active Feedback
The rich saturation of Ruby allows for a more dynamic "Generating" state, using warm-spectrum gradients (Sunset Orange to Ruby) to signal that the Glow is at work. The Champagne (#FBECD6) accent provides soft contrast without competing with the primary Ruby stroke.
